Vocabulary

The seven building blocks

Every trade in the journal is assembled from the same components. Learn to see these on a chart and the rest is sequencing.

🎯

Draw on Liquidity (DOL)

Where price is most likely heading — a previous week high/low, an old swing, an HTF objective. This is your target, and it defines your bias.

PWH · PWL · old highs/lows
📦

Order Block

The last opposing candle before a displacement move. Price often returns to this zone to rebalance before continuing.

H4 / H1 POI
⚡

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

A three-candle imbalance where price moved so fast it left an unfilled gap. Acts as a magnet and a refined entry zone.

Imbalance / inefficiency
💧

Liquidity Sweep

Price spikes through an obvious high or low to trigger stops, then reverses. This is the fuel for the real move.

Stop run
🕯️

CRT — Candle Range Theory

A range forms over one or more candles, price sweeps one side, then delivers to the other.H4, H1, H4+H1, or 1D+4H — the timeframe where the range/sweep model is valid.

H4 · H1 · H4+H1 · 1D+4H
🔄

CISD

Change in State of Delivery — the lower-timeframe confirmation. A displacement candle that breaks the prior delivery direction.

5m / 15m trigger
🧩

Narrative Overlap

When H4 and H1 tell the same story. This is the single biggest conviction booster in the entire model.

H4 + H1 confluence
Execution Flow

Seven phases, top-down

Work through these in order. Never skip a phase to get to the entry — the entry is the last thing that happens, not the first.

01

Bias & Draw on Liquidity

Weekly · Daily · DXY

Start with the biggest picture. Where is price being drawn to, and what is the dollar doing?

  • Check DXY first. Inverse for EURUSD, GBPUSD, XAUUSD. Positive for USDCAD.
  • Locate the DOL. Previous week high/low, old swing, HTF objective.
  • Decide trend or counter-trend. Trend is the default. Counter-trend needs exceptional confluence.
  • Write one sentence. "DXY bullish → USDCAD long toward PWH." If you can't write it, you have no bias.
02

Mark the H4 & H1 Narratives

Points of Interest

Map the zones where you would actually want to do business. This is preparation, not reaction.

  • H4 POI: Order Block, FVG, Swing High/Low, Rejection or Propulsion Block.
  • H1 POI: the refined version of the same idea.
  • Check overlap. H4 + H1 agreeing is the A+ condition. H1 alone is a downgrade.
  • Identify the CRT / range. Mark range high and low — you want one side swept.
03

Wait for Price to Reach the POI

Patience — the hardest phase

Most bad trades are born here. Price sits mid-range and the trader invents an entry.

  • Do not chase. If price is in the middle of the range, you are not trading.
  • Set alerts at your POIs and walk away.
  • Engage only when price enters your H4/H1 zone.
04

Sweep + CISD Trigger

15m / 5m

This is the confirmation engine. No sweep, no trade. No CISD, no trade.

  • Short: price sweeps a high, then 15m bearish displacement closes below the last up-candle's low.
  • Long: price sweeps a low, then 15m bullish displacement closes above the last down-candle's high.
  • Refine: drop to 5m and enter at the OB or FVG created by that displacement.
05

Entry, Stop, Target

Risk defined before you click

Execution is mechanical once the trigger fires. No negotiating with the chart.

  • Entry: CISD close, or a limit at the 5m/15m OB or FVG.
  • Stop: beyond the sweep extreme — the invalidation point, not a round number.
  • Target: the next HTF draw on liquidity. Minimum 2R, prefer 3R+.
  • Risk: 0.5–1% per trade. A loss should always be -1R.
06

Management

Correlation-aware

The journal repeatedly closes early because the dollar finished its own move. That is the model working correctly.

  • If DXY hits its draw, close or aggressively protect the position.
  • Move to break-even only after structure confirms — never on a whim.
  • Exit early if price action degrades, even at a small profit.
  • Never widen the stop. Ever.
07

Journal Every Trade

Bias · Narrative · Execution

The journal is not paperwork — it is the feedback loop that turns screen time into skill.

  • Screenshot three charts: bias, narrative, execution.
  • Record: result, RR, emotions, remarks, mistakes.
  • Review weekly. What was A+? What was FOMO? What did you skip that you should have taken?

Discipline

What the journal teaches

Every loss in the log came from a process break, not a bad model. These are the lines that separate the wins from the losses.

✓  Do This

  • Wait for H4 + H1 overlap before committing size.
  • Require a sweep at the POI before looking for CISD.
  • Take the A+ setup you spent all day waiting for — calmly.
  • Close when DXY hits its draw. Correlation is a timing tool.
  • Journal the emotion, not just the entry and exit.
  • Skip the trade when the stop placement genuinely scares you.

✕  Never This

  • FOMO after a missed entry. The Aug 21 XAUUSD loss was exactly this.
  • Chase a complete reversal from the top or bottom — very low probability.
  • Trade without an H4 narrative at full size.
  • Force the Monday open or thin Asia sessions unless it is genuinely A+.
  • Go counter-trend without correlated-market confirmation.
  • Widen a stop or move it before structure confirms.
