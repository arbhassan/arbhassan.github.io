01 — The Concept, Clarified

What you're actually trading

A POI (Point of Interest) is a price area where institutional orders were left unfilled — visible as a sharp, one-sided displacement away from a small base. Price tends to return to that area to rebalance. But before you even look for a zone, you must know the higher-timeframe direction. Trading a demand POI in a bearish 4H market is how accounts die.

STEP 01

Read 4H & 1H

Decide the bias: bullish, bearish, or ranging. This is your filter for everything else.

STEP 02

Mark the 15m zone

Find a base + strong displacement. Box the origin candle. That box is your POI.

STEP 03

Wait for the return

Do nothing until price comes back and taps the zone. Patience is the edge.

STEP 04

Get 5m proof

Drop to the 5m. Look for rejection, engulfing, MSS or CISD inside the zone.

STEP 05

Enter & manage

Enter on the confirmation close. Stop beyond the zone. Target the opposite POI.

Supply POI

Where sellers are waiting

A small consolidation (base) followed by a violent down move. The last up-candle before that drop is the origin candle. Its body / wick range becomes your sell zone.

Demand POI

Where buyers are waiting

A small consolidation followed by a violent up move. The last down-candle before that rally is the origin candle. Its range becomes your buy zone.

Quality filter

Not all zones are equal

A-grade POIs leave imbalance / FVG, cause a break of structure, sit at a higher-timeframe level, and are fresh — never tested before.

02 — Directional Bias

First, know the trend: 4H & 1H

Before you mark a single zone, open the 4-hour chart. Then the 1-hour. You are answering one question: is this market bullish or bearish right now? Supply & demand only works with the trend — a beautiful demand POI in a downtrend is a trap.

Bullish Bias

Higher Highs · Higher Lows

  • Price is printing higher highs and higher lows
  • The last major break of structure was to the upside
  • Demand POIs hold; supply POIs get broken through
  • Pullbacks into 15m demand are opportunities, not warnings
→ Only look for BUYS at 15m demand POIs
Bearish Bias

Lower Highs · Lower Lows

  • Price is printing lower highs and lower lows
  • The last major break of structure was to the downside
  • Supply POIs hold; demand POIs get broken through
  • Rallies into 15m supply are opportunities, not strength
→ Only look for SELLS at 15m supply POIs
4H — THE BOSS

Major direction

The 4H tells you the dominant trend. This is your non-negotiable filter. If the 4H is bearish, you do not take buy setups. Ever.

1H — THE CONFIRMATION

Current leg

The 1H shows you the active swing. Is price retracing or continuing? This tells you which POI price is likely to reach next.

ALIGNMENT RULE

Both agree = trade

When 4H and 1H point the same way, you have an A+ environment. When they conflict, reduce size drastically — or simply stay out.

⚠ The one rule that saves accounts

Trade with the 4H trend, or don't trade. Counter-trend supply and demand setups work sometimes, but the win rate collapses and the drawdowns get ugly. Your edge is not in catching reversals — it's in joining the direction that is already delivering.

03 — Step Two

Mark the POI on the 15-minute

Now that you know the direction, zoom in. You're not looking for patterns — you're looking for energy. A zone only exists because price left it with force.

  • 1
    Find the displacement. Scan for a candle or a run of 2–4 candles that moves hard in one direction with tiny wicks. That's an imbalance being created.
  • 2
    Find the base. Look immediately to the left of the displacement — 1 to 3 small, overlapping candles. That's the accumulation / distribution before the move.
  • 3
    Box the origin candle. Take the body (open → close) of the last opposing candle before the move. Widen to the wick only if the body is tiny.
  • 4
    Draw the zone forward. Extend the rectangle to the right. It stays valid until price mitigates it.
  • 5
    Grade it. Does it sit at a 1H / 4H level? Did it cause a BOS? Did it leave an FVG? Yes to more = higher probability.
  • 6
    Keep only the best 2–3. A chart with fifteen boxes is a chart with no plan.

04 — Step Three

The 5-minute confirmation triggers

Price has tapped your 15m zone — and the 4H / 1H bias agrees with the direction. Now you drop to the 5-minute and wait. You need at least one of these four to fire. No trigger, no trade — the zone alone is not a signal.

TRIGGER 01

Rejection

Price wicks deep into the zone but closes back outside it. The wick is the footprint of aggressive orders absorbing the move. A close beyond the zone = invalid, walk away.

Best when: the wick sweeps a prior low/high — that's liquidity being taken, not genuine continuation.
TRIGGER 02

Engulfing candle

A full-bodied candle that completely engulfs the body of the previous candle, closing hard in your direction. It signals a sudden shift in order flow right at the zone.

Best when: the engulfing candle closes beyond the zone edge and leaves an FVG behind it.
TRIGGER 03

Market Structure Shift

Inside the zone, price makes a low, then breaks the most recent 5m swing high with a displacement candle. The short-term bearish structure is now broken — buyers are in control.

Best when: the break happens with momentum and the entry is on the retest of the broken swing.
TRIGGER 04

CISD — Change in State of Delivery

Mark the open of the first candle in the last consecutive down-close series. When price closes above that level, delivery has flipped from sell-side to buy-side. Mirror it for shorts.

Best when: the CISD candle also breaks a 5m swing — CISD + MSS stacked is a sniper entry.

⚠ Confirmation is not optional

A 15m POI is a location, not an entry. Price can slice straight through a zone and keep going. The 5m trigger exists purely to shift the odds: you're no longer guessing that the zone will hold — you're reacting to evidence that it already has.

07 — Risk Rules

Invalidation & management

  • ✕
    HTF flip = stop trading that direction. If the 4H structure breaks, every remaining POI in that direction is void. Re-map from scratch.
  • ✕
    Zone broken = zone dead. A 5m close decisively through the zone means the orders are gone. Do not re-enter the same zone hoping for a bounce.
  • ✕
    No trigger, no trade. If price taps the zone and grinds sideways without a 5m confirmation, you skip it. There will be another setup.
  • →
    Move to breakeven once price closes beyond the first 5m opposing structure — or at 1R, whichever comes first.
  • →
    Take partials at Target 1 (opposing POI) and let the remainder run to HTF liquidity with a trailing stop.
  • →
    One trade per zone. If you're stopped out, that zone is spent. Move on to the next marked POI.
  • →
    Risk a fixed percentage — typically 0.5%–1% per idea. Zone quality changes, risk does not.
