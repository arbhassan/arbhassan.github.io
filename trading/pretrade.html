01 · The Model

Bias first. Level second. Trigger last.

Nothing about this model is discretionary in the moment. Each timeframe answers one question, and if any answer is missing, there is no trade. That is the whole edge.

FRACTAL

The same pattern everywhere

C1 / C2 / C3 repeats identically on Daily, 4H, 1H and below. You are never learning a new setup — only reading the same one at a different scale.

BIAS

Daily C2 sets direction

Did the Daily candle sweep PDH or PDL and close back inside? A sweep with SMT on a correlated pair counts too. If it hasn't happened, you wait.

LEVEL

No key level, no trade

Price must be sitting at a Daily FVG, 4H OB, 4H FVG, 4H swing high/low, or a 1H FVG with higher-timeframe order flow behind it.

TRIGGER

CISD on the matching LTF

A change in state of delivery confirms the shift. Daily → 1H, 4H → 15m, 1H → 5m. No CISD, no entry — regardless of how good the level looks.

MANAGE

Hold, then protect

Hold to stop loss, or to the recent range high/low sweep, then move to breakeven. Retracements are part of the plan, not a reason to panic.

FILTER

Context and timing

DXY in sync, no high-impact news, and extra care around 3am and 8am when manipulation is most likely. Don't chase the first move.

02 · The Pattern

C1, C2, C3 — the three-candle story

A big reference candle defines the range. A small candle with a long wick raids one side of it and closes back inside — that is the manipulation and it sets the bias. Then a big candle expands away from the swept side. White body = bullish. Black body = bearish. Wicks are always black.

Bullish

C2 sweeps CRL · C3 expands up

Bearish

C2 sweeps CRH · C3 expands down

What each candle does

CandleRoleWhat it means
C1 Reference A big-bodied candle. Its high and low become CRH and CRL — the range that C2 will raid.
C2 Manipulation Small body, long wick. Sweeps CRH or CRL and closes back inside the range. This sets the bias.
C3 Expansion Big body moving in the direction of the bias, away from the swept side. The move you want to be positioned for.

How it stacks across timeframes

TimeframeWhat you take from it
Daily Daily C2: did it sweep PDH/PDL (or sweep with SMT)? That gives the bias.
4H Where we are inside the Daily move. Key levels: 4H OB, 4H FVG, 4H swing high/low.
1H Refines the 4H. 1H FVG with HTF order flow; checks 4H / 1H alignment.

03 · The Trigger

CISD — Change In State of Delivery

CISD is your permission slip. Price must first reach a valid key level and sweep liquidity there. Then it has to close beyond the run of opposing candles that created the move into the level. That close is the shift in delivery — enter on it, or on the pullback into it.

Bullish CISD

sweep low → close above → entry

Bearish CISD

sweep high → close below → entry

Reading it

StepRule
1. Key levelPrice reaches a valid key level and sweeps liquidity there.
2. CISD Bearish Price closes below the recent bullish candles (the open of that run).

Bullish Price closes above the recent bearish candles (the open of that run).
3. EntryEnter on the CISD close or the pullback into it. Stop loss goes beyond the sweep.

04 · Price Action

Reading the profiles

Before the pattern completes, you read the profile: is price rejecting a level or accepting through it? Rejection leaves wicks. Acceptance leaves closes.

Bearish profile

Rejecting highs + breaking lows

Bullish profile

Rejecting lows + breaking highs

TermMeaning
RejectingA wick above the recent leg highs (or below the recent leg lows): the level is tested, not accepted.
BreakingPrice then trades through the other side of the leg: the lows (bearish) or the highs (bullish).
Close above / belowCloses above the recent leg highs = higher prices. Closes below the recent leg lows = lower prices.

05 · The Process

Five steps, in order, every time

Skip a step and you are no longer trading the model — you are trading a feeling.

01

Daily Bias

Read C1 / C2. Has Daily C2 swept PDH / PDL? A sweep with SMT also counts. If not, wait for the sweep based on current order flow.

02

4H / 1H Alignment

The 4H and 1H C1 / C2 / C3 should agree with the Daily bias. DXY should be in sync with the direction you are about to trade.

03

Key level Location

Price must be at one: Daily FVG, 4H OB, 4H FVG, 4H swing high/low, or 1H FVG with HTF order flow. No key level, no trade.

04

LTF entry Trigger

Wait for CISD on the matching lower timeframe: 1H for Daily, 15m for 4H, 5m for 1H. No CISD, no entry.

05

Execute & manage Discipline

Hold to SL, or to the recent range low/high sweep, then move to breakeven. Trust the system.

06 · Rules

The non-negotiables

Each of these is a filter. Together they are the reason the checklist exists.

RULE 01

Key level present

Daily FVG, 4H OB, 4H FVG, 4H swing H/L, or 1H FVG with HTF order flow. No key level, no trade.

RULE 02

Daily C2 swept PDH / PDL

A sweep with SMT also counts. If it hasn't happened yet, wait for the sweep based on current order flow.

RULE 03

DXY in sync

DXY confirms the direction of the trade. If it disagrees, the setup drops a grade.

RULE 04

Valid CISD on the matching LTF

Daily → 1H, 4H → 15m, 1H → 5m. The entry model is mandatory.

RULE 05

Time check

You can trade any time, but be careful around 3am and 8am — manipulation times. Don't chase the first move.

RULE 06

No high-impact news

Avoid trading when high-impact news is due.

RULE 07

Rejection block (if used)

4H / 1H rejection blocks only count with SMT on a correlated pair.

RULE 08

1H level is FVG

Not a swing high/low — unless the 4H and 1H swings overlap.

RULE 09

Trade management

Hold to SL or to the recent range low/high sweep, then move to BE. Anticipate retracements. Trust the system.

07 · Grades

How good is this setup, really?

Grade the trade before you take it. A+ means full confluence. A means it works but something is missing. No trade means exactly that.

A+

Full confluence.

Daily FVG, 4H OB or 4H FVG, or a 4H swing level with 4H + 1H overlap — plus Daily C2 swept, DXY in sync, and a valid CISD on the matching lower timeframe.

A

Hard stops pass, but softer.

The level is a 1H FVG or a rejection block, or DXY is not in sync, or Daily C2 hasn't swept yet. Tradeable, but with reduced conviction and size.

No trade

Stand down.

No key level, no CISD, high-impact news due, or DXY out of sync and Daily C2 unswept together. There is always another setup.
